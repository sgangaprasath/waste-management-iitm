### Different Categories of Waste Generated

#### Household Waste

##### Kitchen Waste

- **Wet food scraps:** Vegetable peels, fruit rinds, leftover cooked food, expired food items, tea leaves, coffee grounds → *Wet/Biodegradable Waste*  
- **Food packaging:** Plastic containers (takeaway boxes, milk pouches), aluminum foil, glass jars, and bottles → *Dry Waste, recyclable if clean*  
- **Multi-layered packaging:** Milk cartons, snack wrappers, frozen food packages, instant noodle packets → *Non-recyclable Waste*  
- **Cooking oil and grease:** Used cooking oil, oil-soaked paper towels → *Special disposal required*  

##### Living Area Waste

- **Paper-based:** Newspapers, magazines, old books, cardboard boxes, paper bills, and documents → *Recyclable/Biodegradable Dry Waste*  
- **Plastic-based:** Plastic bags, bottles, containers, toys, packaging materials → *Non-biodegradable Dry Waste*  
- **Textiles:** Old clothes, bed sheets, curtains, towels, footwear → *Recyclable or donation-worthy if in good condition*  

##### Bathroom / Personal Care Waste

- **Sanitary waste:** Sanitary napkins, diapers, cotton balls, used tissues, personal hygiene products → *Non-recyclable Waste (wrap properly before disposal)*  
- **Plastic containers:** Shampoo bottles, cosmetic containers, medicine bottles → *Recyclable Dry Waste if clean*  
- **Expired medicines:** Tablets, syrups, medical supplies → *Hazardous Waste (special collection required)*  

##### Cleaning and Maintenance Waste

- **Cleaning supplies:** Empty detergent containers, cleaning chemical bottles, mop heads, old brushes → *Mixed category depending on contamination*  
- **Household repairs:** Broken appliances, old furniture, paint cans, hardware materials → *Bulky or Hazardous Waste*  
- **Garden waste:** Pruned branches, fallen leaves, grass clippings, plant pots → *Biodegradable Waste suitable for composting*  

##### Electronic Waste

- **Small appliances:** Mixers, rice cookers, fans, electronic gadgets, chargers, batteries → *E-waste requiring separate collection*  
- **Old electronics:** Televisions, computers, mobile phones, audio equipment → *E-waste requiring certified disposal*

### Seasonal and Occasional Waste

##### Festival / Celebration Waste

- **Decorative materials:** Flowers, rangoli materials, lighting decorations, thermocol items → *Mixed (biodegradable and non-recyclable)*  
- **Gift packaging:** Wrapping paper, plastic decorations, greeting cards → *Dry Waste, mostly recyclable*  

##### Moving / Relocation Waste

- **Packing materials:** Cardboard boxes, bubble wrap, packing foam, plastic covers → *Recyclable Dry Waste*  
- **Discarded household items:** Furniture, appliances, cycles, books, personal belongings → *Bulky Waste or donation-worthy items*  

##### Construction / Renovation Waste

- **Building materials:** Broken tiles, cement debris, paint cans, tools → *Construction and Demolition Waste*  
- **Old fixtures and replacements:** Bathroom fittings, electrical components, plumbing materials → *Mixed category requiring specialized disposal*

### Disposal Guidelines

| **Waste Type** | **Disposal Guidelines** |
|-----------------|-------------------------|
| **Dry Recyclable Waste** | Dispose in **blue bins**. Rinse containers before disposal to remove residue. Flatten cardboard boxes if too large. Remove or keep bottle caps as per local recycling rules. Ensure paper is clean and dry. |
| **Wet / Biodegradable Waste** | Dispose in **green bins**. Drain liquids before disposal. Use compostable bin liners. Avoid pouring cooking oil directly—collect separately for proper disposal. |
| **Non-Recyclable Waste** | Dispose in **red bins**. Wrap contaminated items in newspaper or plastic bags. Include soiled paper, mixed materials, and multilayered packaging. Seal properly to prevent odour. |
| **Sanitary Waste** | Wrap securely in opaque plastic or newspaper, mark appropriately, and dispose in **designated sanitary bins** or **red bins**. Maintain hygiene during disposal. |
| **Hazardous Waste** | Store separately until special collection drives. Includes expired medicines, paint cans, chemicals, and batteries. Coordinate with campus authorities for disposal. Never dispose in regular bins. |
| **E-Waste** | Deposit at **designated e-waste collection points**. Remove batteries for separate disposal. Never mix with regular waste. |

### Best Practices

#### Infrastructure and Collection Systems

##### Waste Collection Points

- Provide **color-coded bins** with clear signage and multilingual labels for segregation.  
- Ensure **daily collection** of wet waste and **twice-weekly collection** of dry waste from individual quarters.  
- Create **separate collection points for bulky waste** (furniture, appliances) at block level.  
- Set up **secure e-waste collection areas** until pickup by certified recyclers.  
- Ensure **segregated transport**, preventing mixing of different waste streams.  

##### Awareness Programs

- Conduct regular **awareness campaigns** on waste segregation and disposal.  
- Include waste management orientation for **new residents** during onboarding.  
- Distribute printed or digital **segregation guides** in welcome kits.  

##### Community Involvement

- Establish **resident volunteer programs** to assist in monitoring waste activities.  
- Form **waste management committees** at the residential zone level to oversee implementation.  

##### Hazardous Waste

- Organize **monthly collection drives** for hazardous items such as medicines, batteries, and chemical containers.  
- Provide **temporary storage containers** for households to hold hazardous waste safely.  
- Partner with **certified disposal agencies** for treatment and disposal.  

##### Bulky Waste

- Schedule **regular collection drives** for furniture, appliances, and large items, especially during academic breaks.  
- Promote **reuse and donation programs** for items in good condition.  
- Maintain **temporary storage areas** for bulky items awaiting collection.  

##### Quality Control

- Conduct **waste segregation audits** to assess compliance and identify gaps.  
- Enable **feedback mechanisms** for residents to report collection or bin issues.  
- Track **waste generation and diversion rates** to evaluate program success.  

##### Service Optimization

- Conduct **resident satisfaction surveys** periodically.  
- Adjust **collection schedules** based on feedback and waste generation patterns.  
- Provide **adequate bin capacity** based on population density and waste output.
