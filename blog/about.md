# About

This website was built by the joint efforts of the Sustainability Committee, the Engineering Unit and the Waste Management Committee. A special mention to all the student volunteers who took their time to make this platform functional.

The Waste Management Committee of IIT Madras is dedicated to fostering a
cleaner, greener, and more sustainable environment within our institute.
Our mission is to implement effective waste management practices, promote
awareness, and encourage responsible waste disposal among all
stakeholders.

Our committee is comprised of passionate members:
* Prof. Indumathi Nambi
* Prof. Susy Varghese
* Dr. S Ganga Prasath
* Ms. Srividhya
* Ms. Vysakhi

The team works in close collaboration with the Engineering Unit of the
institute, which does the ground-level implementation of the waste
management on campus.
