      ### Disposal Guidelines


      | Waste Type              | Disposal
      Guidelines                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     
      |

      | ----------------------- |
      --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
      |

      | Dry Recyclable Waste    | Dispose of in blue bins. Rinse containers
      before disposal to avoid contamination. Specify the level of rinsing
      required (e.g., just water, or soap). Guidelines on how and where to
      dispose of bulky items like big cardboard boxes. For example, flatten them
      and place them next to the bins or a designated area. Some instructions
      for the plastic caps, that is, to keep them on or to remove them. |

      | Wet/Biodegradable Waste | Dispose of in green bins. Avoid disposing of
      liquids directly into bins without lining. Emphasize draining all liquids
      before disposal. Guidance on using bin liners for wet waste to prevent
      leakage and
      odor.                                                                                                                                                                                         
      |

      | Non-Recyclable Waste    | Dispose of in red bins. Ensure proper wrapping
      of heavily contaminated waste to avoid odor. Mandate the proper wrapping
      of all sanitary napkins in proper opaque bags. Clear instructions for
      broken glass (e.g., wrap in newspaper, label "broken glass" for
      safety).                                                                                                                                  
      |

      ### Best Practices


      1. There should be separate bins for the collection and disposal of
      hazardous wastes and e-waste, which are not there at present.

      2. Encourage hostel residents to minimise single-use plastics by using
      steel or reusable containers.

      3. The vacating students leaving behind the mattress or some plastic
      equipment opt for re-use during internship programs or events. Given to
      the students or staff in need.

      4. Regular waste audits in hostels to track segregation compliance and
      waste generation rates.

      5. Specific numbers and placement of bins on each floor, common areas.
      Taking feedback from the students accordingly - Are the bins sufficient?
      Are they well-maintained?

      6. Monitor waste segregation adherence during hostel events or gatherings.
