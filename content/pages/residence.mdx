      ### Disposal Guidelines:


      | **Waste Type**          | **Disposal
      Guidelines**                                                                                                                                                                                                                             
      |

      | ----------------------- |
      ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
      |

      | Dry Recyclable Waste    | Dispose in blue bins. Rinse containers before
      disposal to remove food residue. Flatten cardboard boxes and place near
      bins if too large. Remove caps from bottles or keep them on based on local
      recycling guidelines. Paper should be clean and dry |

      | Wet/Biodegradable Waste | Dispose in green bins. Drain all liquids
      before disposal. Use compostable bin liners to prevent leakage and odor.
      Avoid disposing of cooking oil directly - collect separately for proper
      disposal                                                   |

      | Non-Recyclable Waste    | Dispose in red bins. Wrap heavily contaminated
      items in newspaper or plastic bags. Multi-layered packaging, soiled
      papers, and mixed materials go here. Ensure proper sealing to prevent
      odour                                                       |

      | Sanitary Waste          | Securely wrap in opaque plastic bags or
      newspaper and mark with appropriate indicators. Dispose in designated
      sanitary waste bins or with non-recyclable waste in red bins. Maintain
      hygiene during disposal.                                        |

      | Hazardous Waste         | Store separately in household until collection
      drives. Include expired medicines, paint cans, chemical containers,
      batteries. Coordinate with campus authorities for special collection
      events. Never dispose in regular bins.                       |

      | E-Waste                 | Collect in designated e-waste collection
      points. Remove batteries separately and dispose in battery collection
      points. Never mix with regular
      waste.                                                                                                
      |

      ## Waste Collection Points


      * Individual housing units

      * Apartments: separate waste collection from each house

      * Community areas: Community Hall, Children’s play area, Shopping Complex,
      Temples

      * Community Gardens and roadside bins

      * Schools (Kendriya Vidyalaya, Vana Vani)

      * Service Areas: Include maintenance areas, vehicle parking zones, and
      administrative offices specific to the residential zone.


      ## Different Categories of Waste Generated


      ### Household Waste:


      #### Kitchen Waste:


      * Wet food scraps: Vegetable peels, fruit rinds, leftover cooked food,
      expired food items, tea leaves, coffee grounds → Wet/Biodegradable Wastes

      * Food packaging: Plastic containers (takeaway boxes, milk pouches),
      aluminum foil, glass jars and bottles → Dry Waste, but Recyclable only if
      clean

      * Multi-layered packaging: Milk cartons, snack wrappers, frozen food
      packages, instant noodle packets → Non-Recyclable Wastes

      * Cooking oil and grease: Used cooking oil, oil-soaked paper towels →
      Special Disposal Required


      #### Living Area Waste:


      * Paper-based: Newspapers, magazines, old books, cardboard boxes from
      online deliveries, paper bills, and documents → Highly
      Recyclable/Biodegradable Dry Wastes

      * Plastic-based: Plastic bags, bottles, containers, toys, household items,
      packaging materials → Non-biodegradable dry Wastes

      * Textiles: Old clothing, bed sheets, curtains, towels, footwear →
      Recyclable or Donation-worthy if in good condition


      #### Bathroom/Personal Care Waste:


      * Sanitary waste: Sanitary napkins, diapers, cotton balls, used tissues,
      personal hygiene products → Non-Recyclable Wastes requiring proper
      wrapping

      * Plastic containers: Shampoo bottles, cosmetic containers, medicine
      bottles → Recyclable Dry Wastes if clean

      * Expired medicines: Tablets, syrups, medical supplies → Hazardous Waste
      requiring special collection


      #### Cleaning and Maintenance:


      * Cleaning supplies: Empty detergent containers, cleaning chemical
      bottles, mop heads, old brushes → Mixed category depending on material and
      contamination

      * Household repairs: Broken appliances, old furniture, paint cans,
      hardware materials → Bulky Waste or Hazardous Waste

      * Garden waste: Pruned branches, fallen leaves, grass clippings, plant
      pots → Biodegradable Waste suitable for composting


      #### Electronic Waste:


      * Small appliances: Mixers, rice cookers, fans, electronic gadgets,
      chargers, batteries → E-Waste requiring separate collection

      * Old electronics: Televisions, computers, mobile phones, audio equipment
      → E-Waste requiring certified disposal


      ## Seasonal and Occasional Waste


      #### Festival/Celebration Waste:


      * Decorative materials: Flowers, rangoli materials, lighting decorations,
      thermocol items → Mixed - Biodegradable and Non-Recyclable

      * Gift packaging: Wrapping paper, plastic decorations, greeting cards →
      Dry Waste mostly recyclable


      #### Moving/Relocation Waste:


      * Packing materials: Cardboard boxes, bubble wrap, packing foam, plastic
      covers → Recyclable Dry Wastes

      * Discarded household items: Furniture, appliances, cycles, books,
      personal belongings → Bulky Waste or Donation-worthy items


      #### Construction/Renovation Waste:


      * Building materials: Broken tiles, cement debris, paint cans,
      construction tools → Construction and Demolition Waste

      * Old fixtures and replacements: Bathroom fittings, electrical components,
      plumbing materials → Mixed category requiring specialized disposal
